Health Awareness

Understanding global health challenges is the first step toward change. Explore the
critical issues affecting millions worldwide.

Cardiovascular disease17.9M deaths/year

Heart disease is the #1 killer worldwide. High blood pressure, cholesterol,and smoking are major risk factors. Regular exercise and a healthy diet can reduce your risk by up to 80%.

💚

Tobacco & Substance Abuse 8M deaths/year

Tobacco kills more than 8 million people each year. It causes cancer, heart disease, and lung disease.

🚬

Maternal & Child Health 295K deaths/year

Every day, approximately 800 women die from preventable pregnancy-related causes.

👶

Vaccination 3.5–5M lives saved/year

Vaccines prevent millions of deaths every year and protect against serious diseases.

💉

Clean Water & Sanitation2B people affected

Contaminated water and poor sanitation cause diseases like cholera, diarrhea, and typhoid. 2 billion people still lack safely managed drinking water services.

💦

HIV/AIDS38.4M living with HIV

38.4 million people globally live with HIV. with antiretroviral treatment, people with HIV can live long, healthy lives. Awareness and testing are key to prevention.

🫀

Malaria & Tropical Disease600k+ malaria deaths/year

Malaria alone kills over 600,000 people annually, mostly children under 5 in Africa. Bed nets, indoor spraying, and antimalarial drugs save million of lives.

🍾

Air Pollution7M premature deaths/year

Air pollution causes 7 million premature deaths annually. Both outdoor and indoor pollution contribute to stroke, heart disease, and lung cancer. clean energy solutions are essential.

🚸
