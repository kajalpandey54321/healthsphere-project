🌱Small Steps to Better Health

🏃‍♂️Regular Exercise

Stay active by doing at least 30 minutes of exercise daily. It helps improve your physical fitness, boosts energy.

🥗 Healthy Diet

Eat a balanced diet that includes fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and proteins. A healthy diet keeps your body strong and boosts immunity.

🧘‍♀️ Mental Wellness

Take care of your mind by practicing meditation, staying positive, and managing stress.

😴 Proper Sleep

Get 7–8 hours of quality sleep every night. Proper rest helps your body recover and improves concentration and mood.

🧼 Hygiene

Maintain personal cleanliness by washing hands regularly and keeping your surroundings clean. Good hygiene prevents diseases.

💧Stay Hydrated

Drink enough water daily to keep your body active, improve digestion, and maintain healthy skin.

About SDG 3

SDG 3 (Good Health and Well-Being) focuses on ensuring healthy lives and promoting well-being for all people at all ages. It aims to reduce diseases, improve healthcare services, and make quality medical facilities accessible to everyone.

Our Mission

Our mission is to spread awareness about health, encourage healthy lifestyles, and support access to better healthcare for all. We aim to build a healthier society by educating and empowering people.

Health Challenges
